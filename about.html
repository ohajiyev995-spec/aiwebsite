About the Compendium

A fan-built archive of the Nine Titans and every inheritor they have ever had in the story.

Purpose

The Titans Compendium is a spoiler-aware reference for fans who want quick lore overviews without diving into full episode summaries. It highlights each Titan’s defining traits, powers, limitations, and known inheritors.

How it was built

This site is handcrafted with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Data lives in a single JSON file, so the same information powers every page. When network access is unavailable, lightweight inline fallbacks keep the featured Titans and directory functional.

Accessibility

Keyboard users can tab through every interactive element, open cards with Enter or Space, and close dialogs with Escape. Focus outlines are always visible, motion respects the user’s reduced-motion setting, and the modal traps focus for screen reader users.
